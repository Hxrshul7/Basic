Vikramashila: What Was Studied

Subjects

  • Vajrayana Buddhism - the speciality of Vikramashila
  • Philosophy - Madhyamaka and Yogacara schools
  • Logic - scholars like Jnanashrimitra and Ratnakirti wrote on it
  • Grammar - the base for studying every other subject
  • Translation - Sanskrit texts were translated into Tibetan

Legacy

Vikramashila linked Indian Buddhism with Tibet. Teachers like Atisha helped shape Tibetan Buddhism, and many Sanskrit works survive today in Tibetan translation. The monastery declined around 1200 CE and was rediscovered by excavation in the 1960s.
