Nalanda: What Was Studied

Subjects

  • Buddhist philosophy - the main subject; Xuanzang studied here under the teacher Shilabhadra
  • Logic - the science of correct reasoning and debate
  • Grammar - Sanskrit grammar was the base of all study
  • Medicine - mentioned in the travellers' accounts
  • Vedas and other schools - studied so that students could debate them

Legacy

Nalanda became a model of a large residential place of learning. Its ruins today show rows of brick monasteries and temples, and they remind us how knowledge travelled between India, China and the rest of Asia. The site declined and was abandoned around the year 1200 CE.
