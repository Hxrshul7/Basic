Takshashila: What Was Studied

Subjects

  • Vedas - learned from a master teacher
  • Grammar - tradition connects the grammarian Panini with this region
  • Medicine - tradition says the physician Jivaka trained here
  • Archery and war arts - taught to princes in the old stories
  • Statecraft - tradition links Chanakya (Kautilya) with Takshashila

Legacy

Takshashila shows that learning in ancient India was not only in big monasteries. The ruins of three cities (Bhir Mound, Sirkap and Sirsukh) and Buddhist monasteries such as Dharmarajika and Jaulian can still be seen. Some stories about its famous students are traditions, not proven history.
