Vikramashila

A royal Buddhist monastery of the Pala kings and a bridge to Tibet.

Location: Antichak, Bhagalpur district, Bihar, India
Period: about 8th/9th century CE to about 1200 CE

The excavated monastery at Vikramashila

About Vikramashila

Vikramashila was a Buddhist monastery-university built in the time of the Pala dynasty. Tibetan tradition says it was founded by King Dharmapala.

It was about 190 km east of Nalanda and became an important centre of Vajrayana Buddhism. Excavations at Antichak have uncovered a huge square monastery with a large stupa in the middle.

The monastery and central stupa seen from above
