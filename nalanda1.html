About Nalanda

Nalanda was a large Buddhist monastery (called a mahavihara) near Rajgir in Bihar. For about seven hundred years it was one of the biggest centres of learning in the ancient world.

Students and teachers lived there together. Chinese travellers such as Xuanzang and Yijing studied at Nalanda in the 7th century CE and wrote about what they saw.
