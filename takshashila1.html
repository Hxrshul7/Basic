Takshashila

An ancient city of teachers on the trade routes of Asia. Also called Taxila.

Location: Ancient Gandhara (now Punjab, Pakistan)
Period: about 5th century BCE to 5th century CE

Dharmarajika monastery at Taxila

About Takshashila

Takshashila (Taxila) was a famous place of learning long before Nalanda was founded. It was not one single campus. Students came to the city and studied with individual teachers.

It stood on trade routes joining India with Central Asia and Persia, so many cultures met here. Old Buddhist Jataka stories describe young students travelling to Takshashila to learn.
