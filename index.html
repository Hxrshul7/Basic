The Ancient Academies

Where knowledge crossed borders.

India had great centres of learning thousands of years ago. This project is about three of them: Takshashila, Nalanda and Vikramashila. Click a name to read about it.

Takshashila is the oldest. It existed before Nalanda. Vikramashila came later, under the Pala kings.

Takshashila

Ancient Gandhara (now Pakistan). About 5th century BCE to 5th century CE. A city of teachers where students learned medicine, grammar, politics and war arts.

Read about Takshashila

Nalanda

Bihar, India. About 5th to 12th century CE. A huge Buddhist monastery-university known for philosophy, logic and medicine.

Read about Nalanda

Vikramashila

Bihar, India. About 8th/9th to 12th century CE. A Pala-era Buddhist monastery with a strong link to Tibet.
